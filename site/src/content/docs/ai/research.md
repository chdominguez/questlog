Get a course you can check. Questie searches the web, reads the best sources and builds a course where every theory card links to the pages it is based on. It costs 200 credits.

## Build it

1. Press **Build** in the top bar and open the **Web research** tab.
2. Type a **Topic**, for example `Rust ownership and borrowing`.
3. Pick a **Level**: **Beginner**, **Intermediate** or **Advanced**.
4. Press **Build course · 200**, then confirm **Spend 200 credits?** with **Build**.

As with topic courses, a topic that is too broad gets **Topic too broad** and suggestions before anything is charged.

## Watch it being built

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Researching | Questie searches and reads the web; a counter shows the sources found, like `14 sources` |
| Outline | The levels are planned from the research |
| Writing levels | Levels are written from the sources |
| Checking | The course is validated and every citation is checked against the sources Questie actually read |
| Saving | The course is saved to your account |

**Course ready** shows the size of the course plus the number of sources, for example `23 sources`.

## Citations on every card

Every theory card in a research course carries its sources:

- Numbered markers in the text, like `[1]`, jump to the source list.
- **Sources**, under the card, lists each source with its title and domain, opening in a new tab.
- The **Materials** tab of the course gathers every source per level in one list.

Questie only cites pages it read during the research, so every link leads to a real source. Each research course stands on at least 3 distinct sources. In the library, research courses carry a **Cited** tag.

## If a build fails

If the research can't find enough sources, or anything else fails, your 200 credits come back automatically (**200 credits refunded**). Press **Edit** to adjust the topic, or **Try again**.

## Which mode to pick

| You want | Mode | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| A course on a topic, fast | [Topic](/ai/topic/) | 100 (first one free) |
| A course on your own material | [Files](/ai/files/) | 150 |
| A course with every claim linked to a source | Web research | 200 |

All three share the same builder, the same checks and the same [share and publish](/ai/publish/) options. See [Credits](/learn/credits/) for plans and top-ups.
