Type what you want to learn, pick your level, and Questie writes a full course: theory cards, mixed challenge types and katas, ready to play. It costs 100 credits, and your first one is free.

## Build it

1. Press **Build** in the top bar (new accounts see the same form on **What do you want to learn?**).
2. Stay on the **Topic** tab.
3. Type a **Topic**, for example `Rust ownership and borrowing` (3 to 200 characters).
4. Pick a **Level**: **Beginner**, **Intermediate** or **Advanced**.
5. Press **Build course · Free** for your first course, or **Build course · 100** after that.
6. For a paid build, confirm **Spend 100 credits?** and press **Build**.

The more specific the topic, the sharper the course. If a topic is too broad, Questie shows **Topic too broad** with suggestions before anything is charged. Tap one to use it.

## Watch it being built

The build page shows each step as it happens:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Outline | Questie plans the levels; the outline appears on screen |
| Writing levels | Levels are written, with a counter like `3 / 8` |
| Checking | Every level is validated against the course schema and fixed when needed |
| Saving | The course is saved to your account |

You can leave while it runs. A card on **Courses** shows the progress and takes you back. You run one build at a time.

## What you get

**Course ready** shows what was built, for example `8 levels · 64 challenges · 6 katas · 9 challenge types`. Press **Start** to open the map.

Every AI course passes the same schema checks as the hand-written library courses. Questie rejects courses made only of multiple choice, so you get a real mix, such as ordering, find-the-bug, fill-the-blanks, written answers and katas. See [Challenges](/learn/challenges/).

Your course starts private. Share it by link or submit it to the library from [Share and publish](/ai/publish/).

## If a build fails

You see **Build failed** and your credits come back automatically (**100 credits refunded**). A free build gives your free course back (**Free course restored**). Press **Try again**, or **Edit** to change the topic first.
