Questie is run from the European Union, and this policy follows the GDPR. It covers the hosted app at app.questie.app, the iOS and Android apps, and this website. A self-hosted copy of Questie is run by whoever hosts it, not by us.

## What we keep

| Data | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Account: email, name, sign-in method | To sign you in and send account emails |
| Learning data: progress, stars, XP, review schedule, written answers and their grades | To run the app and sync it across your devices |
| Notes and your courses | To show them to you, and to others only when you share or publish a course |
| Files, notes and links you give the course builder | To build the course you asked for |
| Credits: balance, grants, spends and refunds | To run the credits system and show your history |
| Purchases: plan status and purchase ids | To grant credits and manage your plan |
| Device push tokens and reminder times | To send the reminders you turned on |

We do not store card numbers. Payments are handled by Stripe on the web and by the App Store or Google Play in the apps.

## Who processes data for us

| Service | What it does | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anthropic | AI course building, grading and stronger answers | Course requests, the files, notes and links you give the builder, and written answers you send for grading |
| ElevenLabs | Narrated lessons | Lesson text |
| Stripe | Web payments, VAT and invoices | Your email and payment details |
| RevenueCat | In-app purchases | Your account id and store purchase records |
| Resend | Account emails | Your email address |
| Apple, Google | Sign in with Apple or Google, push notifications | What their sign-in and push services need |

Files you upload to the course builder are sent to Anthropic for that build and deleted from Anthropic's file storage within 24 hours.

## Legal basis

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for (contract), to keep the service secure and working (legitimate interest), and to meet tax and accounting duties (legal obligation).

## Cookies

The website sets no tracking cookies. The app uses sign-in cookies on the web and secure device storage in the apps.

## How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is deleted, including your public courses. Payment records are kept as long as tax law requires.

## Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data, object to processing, and complain to your data protection authority.

- **Export:** download your progress, notes, answers and courses from the app. See [Export your data](/self-host/export/).
- **Delete:** **Profile**, then **Delete account**, on the web or in the apps.
