Each level is a short run: read the theory, then clear the challenges before you run out of lives. Clear it and you earn stars, XP and the next level.

## A level, step by step

1. Open a level from the map. Choose **Theory** to read first, or **Challenge** to jump straight in.
2. Step through the theory cards with **Previous** and **Next** (or the arrow keys). The last card has **Start challenges**.
3. Answer the challenges in order. A bar at the top shows how far you are.
4. Clear the last one and you see **Level cleared**, your stars and the XP you earned.

## Lives

You start every level with 3 lives.

- A wrong answer on a quiz, select-all, true/false, order, find-the-bug, match, fill-the-blanks or sort challenge costs one life.
- A partial answer costs no life.
- Written answers, flashcards and katas never cost a life.
- Lose all three and you see **Out of lives**. Press **Retry** to start the level again, or **Review theory** to read the cards first.

Lives refill at the start of every run. They never cost money and never run on a timer.

## Stars

Stars equal the lives you have left when you clear the level: 3 lives, 3 stars. The map keeps your best result, so a replay can only raise it. Under 3 stars, the result screen offers **Retry**.

## XP

Every right answer earns XP. Harder challenge types earn more.

| Challenge | Base XP |
|---|---|
| Flashcard | 8 |
| Quiz | 10 |
| Select all, order, find the bug, match, sort | 15 |
| Fill the blanks | 20 |
| Written answer | 25 |
| Kata | 40 |

- A partial answer earns half.
- A challenge you already cleared earns 2 XP on replays, so ranks reflect what you learned.
- Answers in [review](/learn/review/) and the [arcade](/learn/games/) earn XP too.

## Combos

Right answers in a row build a combo, shown by the flame in the top bar.

| Streak | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| 3 in a row | x1.5 |
| 5 in a row | x2 |

A partial or wrong answer resets the combo to zero.

## Ranks

Your total XP sets your rank. The bar next to it shows how close the next one is.

| Rank | XP |
|---|---|
| Novice | 0 |
| Apprentice | 150 |
| Adept | 400 |
| Expert | 800 |
| Master | 1,300 |
| Sage | 1,900 |

## The boss level

The boss level always sits last on the map and opens when you clear the level before it. Clearing it shows **Final level cleared**. In many courses the boss also feeds the [mock exam](/learn/mock/).

Each challenge type is explained in [Challenges](/learn/challenges/).
