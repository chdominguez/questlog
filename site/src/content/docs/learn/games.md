Practice does not have to feel like homework. Every course comes with arcade games, drills and inline practice, all built from the course itself and all free.

## Arcade

Open the **Arcade** tab of a course. The games use the challenges from levels you have unlocked, so they grow as you go. Your best score or time for each game is saved.

| Game | How it plays | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Speed Quiz | 10 questions, 20 seconds each. Tap a tile or press 1 to 4. | Up to 1,000 points per answer, more for faster answers, times your combo |
| Match Race | Match pairs against the clock. | Your time; each wrong match adds 1 second |
| Order Rush | 5 rounds of putting steps in order. | Your time; each wrong order adds 2 seconds |
| Falling | Type the answer before the prompt hits the ground. Each one falls faster. | Prompts cleared, with 3 lives |

A game opens once the course has enough material for it (for example, 4 questions for Speed Quiz or 3 pairs for Match Race). Unlock more levels to open the rest.

Arcade answers count: they earn XP and update your [review](/learn/review/) schedule. Games never cost lives in a level.

## Drills

Some theory cards end in a match drill, right after a table or a list of terms. It builds up one pair at a time: match one pair, then two, then three, with a new pair each round, then all of them together. New pairs carry a **New** tag. Press **Again** to repeat the drill.

Drills are warm-ups. They are not recorded and cost nothing.

## Inline practice

Some theory cards include a quick challenge (a quiz, select all, order, match, sort or fill the blanks) right after the idea it checks. You get the same feedback as in a level. Press **Again** to retry.

Inline practice is not recorded and costs no lives, so you can test yourself before the real challenges start.
