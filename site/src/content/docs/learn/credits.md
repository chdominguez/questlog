Credits pay for the AI features. Everything else in Questie is free: every library course, all games, katas, review and notes. Every AI button shows its cost before you press it, so you always know what you spend.

## What costs credits

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Course from a topic and level | 100 (your first one is free) |
| Course from files, notes, a URL or a repo | 150 |
| Course from web research, with citations | 200 |
| Grade a written answer | 2 |
| Stronger answer + corrections (after a grade) | 3 |
| Mock exam session | 20 |
| Narrated lesson (once per lesson and voice) | 10 |

The cost appears on the button itself, for example **Grade · 2** or **Build course · 150**. Spends of 100 credits or more ask first: **Spend 150 credits?** with your balance after.

## Get credits

| Option | Price | Credits | Expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| Questie plan | €11.99 / month | 1,000 every month | Reset each billing month |
| Top-up 500 | €5.99 | 500 | Never expire |
| Top-up 1,500 | €14.99 | 1,500 | Never expire |

Prices include VAT. Free users can buy top-ups too, no plan needed. Full details on [Pricing](/pricing/).

## Spend order

Plan credits go first, top-up credits after. Your top-ups stay safe for when you need them.

Plan credits reset each billing month and do not roll over. At renewal you get a fresh 1,000.

## Refunds

When an AI job fails (a build, a grade, a stronger answer or narration audio), Questie refunds the credits automatically and right away. You see it on the screen, for example **Build failed** with **150 credits refunded**, and as a row in your history. A failed free course gives your free course back.

## Balance and history

The credit chip in the top bar shows your balance. Press it, or go to **Profile** and **History**, to open **Credits**:

- Your balance, split into plan and top-up credits, and the date plan credits reset.
- Every grant, spend and refund by month, each row naming the action: `Course: <title>`, `Grade`, `Mock exam: <course>`, `Narration: <level>`, `Refund: build failed`, `Top-up`, `Monthly credits` and so on.

When a spend leaves you under 100 credits, a notice offers **Top up**, once per billing period.

## Running low

If you press an AI button without enough credits, the **Not enough credits** sheet shows what you need and what you have, with the plan and both top-ups one tap away. After checkout you come back to the same screen with your work kept, then press the button again.

## Manage your plan

**Profile** shows your plan and renewal date. **Manage** opens the Stripe billing portal to change your card, see invoices or cancel. A canceled plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Plans bought in the app are managed in the App Store or Google Play (see [Mobile apps](/learn/mobile/)).

Self-hosting? With your own API keys and no Stripe keys, billing is off and every AI feature is unmetered with your own key. See [Self-host with Docker](/self-host/docker/).
