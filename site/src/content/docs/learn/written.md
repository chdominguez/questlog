Written answers make you explain an idea in your own words, the best test of whether you understand it. Questie's AI grader scores each answer against the course's model answer and key points, and tells you exactly what you covered and what you missed.

## Answer

Type in the box, or press the microphone to dictate (see below). Some questions show a timer. It is a soft limit: past zero it counts overtime and never cuts your answer off.

## Grade · 2 credits

Press **Grade · 2** (or Ctrl+Enter / Cmd+Enter). You get:

| Part | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Score | 0 to 10 |
| Covered | Key points your answer got right |
| Missing | Key points you left out |
| Mistakes | Statements that are wrong or risky |
| Model answer | The course's reference answer and explanation |

Each course sets its own grader: the role, how strict to be and the language. The HTTP course, for example, grades like a senior web engineer checking your answer against the RFCs.

| Score | Counts as |
|---|---|
| 7 to 10 | Right, full XP (25) |
| 4 to 6 | Partial, half XP |
| 0 to 3 | A miss, no life lost, back in [review](/learn/review/) right away |

## Stronger answer · 3 credits

After a grade, press **Stronger answer · 3** to get:

- **Stronger answer**: your answer rewritten to cover every key point, in your own voice and the course language.
- **Corrections**: each weak phrase from your answer, quoted, next to the fix.

It is a separate choice, so you pay for it only when you want it. You can ask once per graded attempt.

## Free options

- **Show model answer** shows the reference answer, then you rate yourself: **Got it**, **Partly** or **Missed it**.
- Inside a paid [mock exam](/learn/mock/), the button reads **Grade** and every grade is included in the exam price.

## Dictation

Press the microphone and speak. Your words land where the cursor is, in the course language, and you can mix typing and speaking. Press it again to stop. Dictation uses your browser's speech recognition and shows the mic when your browser supports it.

## Your answers, kept

Every attempt is saved with its score, stronger answer and corrections. Open **Previous attempts** under the question, or the **Answers** tab of the course for all of them. **Export .md** downloads them as one Markdown file.

## Refunds

If grading fails, the 2 credits come back at once and you can rate yourself instead. If a stronger answer fails, the 3 credits come back and you can press **Retry**. Refunds show in your [credit history](/learn/credits/).

Self-hosting? With your own `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, grading is unmetered with your own key. See [Configuration](/self-host/config/).
