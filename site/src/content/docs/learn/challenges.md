Questie has 11 challenge types, so you practice recall, reasoning, reading code and writing code, not just picking from a list. Every answer gets instant feedback and an explanation where the course gives one.

## Three possible results

| Result | XP | Lives | Combo | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Right | Full | Kept | Grows | Comes back in days |
| Partial | Half | Kept | Resets | Comes back sooner |
| Wrong | None | Costs one (most types) | Resets | Due again right away |

Which types cost a life is covered in [Levels, XP and lives](/learn/levels/#lives).

## The types

| Type | How you play | Graded |
|---|---|---|
| Quiz | Pick the one right option. Options are shuffled. | Right or wrong |
| True / false | Decide whether a statement holds. | Right or wrong |
| Select all | Pick every right option. | Partial credit (below) |
| Put in order | Drag the steps, or use the up and down buttons, into the right order. | Right only when the whole order matches |
| Find the bug | Read the code and tap the one wrong line. | Right or wrong |
| Match | Link each item on the left to its partner on the right. | Partial credit (below) |
| Fill the blanks | Type into each blank, or tap a word from the chips. | Partial credit (below) |
| Sort | Drop each item into the right bucket. | Partial credit (below) |
| Flashcard | Press **Reveal** (or Space), then rate yourself. | Your rating ([Review](/learn/review/)) |
| Written answer | Explain in your own words, typed or dictated. | Score from 0 to 10 ([Written answers](/learn/written/)) |
| Kata | Write code that passes the tests. | Right when every test passes ([Katas](/learn/katas/)) |

## Partial credit rules

| Type | Right | Partial | Wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Select all | Exactly the right set | No wrong picks, and at least half of the right ones | Anything else |
| Match | Every pair right | 1 or 2 wrong pairs, and fewer than half wrong | Anything else |
| Fill the blanks | Every blank right | At least half the blanks right | Fewer than half |
| Sort | Every item in its bucket | At least half the items right | Fewer than half |
| Written answer | Score 7 to 10 | Score 4 to 6 | Score 0 to 3 (no life lost) |

Fill-the-blanks answers ignore upper and lower case and extra spaces, unless the course marks a blank as exact (code, for example).

## Flashcard ratings

| Button | Key | Counts as |
|---|---|---|
| Again | 1 | Wrong, back in review right away, no life lost |
| Hard | 2 | Partial |
| Good | 3 | Right |
| Easy | 4 | Right, and the next review waits longer |

## After you answer

You see whether you were right, the correct answer for order and match challenges, and the course explanation. Press **Next** to move on. On the last challenge the button reads **See result**.

Every answer you give, in a level, in review or in the arcade, schedules that challenge in your [review queue](/learn/review/).
