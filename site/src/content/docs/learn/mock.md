A mock exam puts you under real exam conditions: a clock, a set of written questions and a grade for each answer. One session costs 20 credits, and every grade inside it is included.

## Start an exam

1. Open a course that has a mock exam. Its button sits at the top of the course map, named after the exam.
2. The intro shows the number of questions and the time, for example `10 questions · 30:00`.
3. Press **Start · 20**. The clock starts.

The questions are the written challenges of the boss level. When the boss has none, Questie picks up to 8 written challenges from across the course.

## During the exam

- Answer each question, typed or [dictated](/learn/written/#dictation).
- Press **Grade** to get the score, covered and missing points. No extra credits.
- Press **Next** to move on, **Finish** on the last question.
- The clock in the corner turns urgent in the last minute.

Reloading the page resumes the same session with the same clock. You are never charged twice.

## When time runs out

The exam ends and anything you typed is saved. The results screen shows:

- Your average score as a ring.
- How many questions you answered.
- The score for each question.

Press **Answers** to read every attempt in full.

## Cost and refunds

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Start a mock exam session | 20 |
| Grade an answer inside the session | Included |

If the exam fails on our side before your first grade, you see **Exam failed · 20 credits refunded** and can press **Try again**. See [Credits](/learn/credits/).

Course authors add a mock exam with the `mock` field. See [Grader and mock exam](/authoring/grader-mock/).
