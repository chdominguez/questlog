Questie runs as a native app on iOS and Android. Sign in with the same account and you get the same courses, progress, notes and credit balance you have on the web.

## Sign in

Use the same method you use on the web:

- **Email and password**, on iOS and Android.
- **Continue with Google**, through the native Google sheet, on iOS and Android.
- **Continue with Apple**, through the native Apple sheet, on iOS (listed first there) and on the web.

Links in emails (verify, reset password) open straight in the app when it is installed. Your session is kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).

## One balance everywhere

Credits live in your account, not on a device. Buy on the web and spend on your phone, or the other way round. The balance updates as soon as the purchase lands.

## Buy credits in the app

In the app you buy through the App Store or Google Play:

| Option | Credits |
|---|---|
| Questie plan, monthly | 1,000 every month |
| Top-up | 500 |
| Top-up | 1,500 |

Credit amounts match the web. Prices show in your store's currency, at the store price nearest to the web price.

1. Press **Subscribe** or a top-up, or reach the sheet from any AI button when you run low.
2. Confirm in the store sheet.
3. Questie shows **Adding credits…**, then **Credits added** or **Subscribed**.

On iOS, **Restore purchases** restores what you bought with your Apple ID. If you already subscribe on the web, the app offers top-ups only, so you never pay for the plan twice. The same works the other way round.

Manage an in-app plan with **Manage** in **Profile**, which opens your store subscriptions.

## Built for the phone

- **Narration** keeps playing with the screen locked. See [Narrated lessons](/learn/narration/).
- **Reminders** arrive as native notifications at the times you set in **Profile**.
- **Dictation** in written answers uses the microphone, after you allow it.
- **Files** for the course builder come from the system document picker.
- **Offline**, practice keeps working. AI buttons wait until you are back online.

## Delete your account in the app

**Profile**, then **Delete account**, works in the app as on the web. See [Account](/learn/account/).
