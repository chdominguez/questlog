Keep your own notes right next to the course you are studying. Notes are Markdown, saved to your account, and ready to become a course when you want one.

## Write a note

1. Open a course and choose the **Notes** tab.
2. Press **New note**.
3. Give it a **Title**, add **Tags** (comma separated) and write the body in Markdown.
4. Switch between **Edit** and **Preview** to check how it looks.
5. Press **Save note** (or Ctrl+S / Cmd+S).

Open a note to edit it again, or press **Delete note** to remove it.

Notes render Markdown the same way the course theory does: headings, lists, links and code.

## Find a note

- Type in **Search** to match words in titles and bodies.
- Tap a tag chip to show only notes with that tag. Tap it again to clear the filter.

A note you write in a course belongs to that course. Notes with no course show in every course, so general notes are always close at hand.

## Notes to course

Your notes are the best source for a course about what you need to learn.

| Way | How |
|---|---|
| In the app | Add your notes as Markdown or text files on the **Files** tab of the builder. Questie builds a course from them for 150 credits. See [Course from your files](/ai/files/). |
| With Claude Code or MCP | Connect the Questie MCP server. Claude reads your notes with `note_search` and `note_get` and writes a course that keeps your terms and quotes. See [MCP server](/authoring/mcp/) and [Claude Code plugin](/authoring/claude-code/). |

Courses built from your notes start private. Share them only when you choose to (see [Share and publish](/ai/publish/)).

## Your notes are yours

Notes sync across your devices and the [mobile apps](/learn/mobile/). To take them with you, see [Export your data](/self-host/export/).
