A free account keeps your progress, answers, notes and credits in one place, on every device.

## Sign-in methods

| Method | Where |
|---|---|
| Email and password (8+ characters) | Web, iOS, Android |
| Continue with Google | Web, iOS, Android |
| Continue with Apple | Web, iOS |

With email and password, Questie sends a link to confirm your address. Open it and you are signed in. If it does not arrive, press **Resend email** on the **Check your email** screen. Google and Apple accounts are ready at once.

If you signed up with Google or Apple, keep using that button. Questie reminds you which method your email uses, for example **Use Google to sign in**.

## Forgot your password

1. On **Sign in**, press **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email and press **Send link**.
3. Open the link, choose a new password and press **Save password**.

## Profile

**Profile** holds your name and profile details, your plan and credits, reminders and the **Account** section with **Sign out** and **Delete account**.

Signing out and back in on the same account keeps all your progress.

## Coming from Questlog

Sign in with the same email you used before and confirm it. Your progress, answers and notes attach to your new account on first sign-in.

## Export your data

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Written answers, with scores and feedback | **Answers** tab of a course, **Export .md** |
| A course you built | **Share** on its map, **Download file** (JSON) |
| Everything, on your own server | [Export your data](/self-host/export/) |

A downloaded course file opens in the MCP server and the Claude Code plugin, so you can edit it and upload it again with **Upload new version**. See [Share and publish](/ai/publish/).

## Delete your account

1. Go to **Profile** and press **Delete account**.
2. Type your email to confirm.
3. Press **Delete account**.

This deletes your progress, courses, answers, notes and credits, and it can't be undone. Courses you published to the library are removed too.

- An active web plan is canceled at once.
- A plan bought in the App Store must be canceled there first. The dialog shows **Manage** to open your subscriptions.

Account deletion works the same on the web and in the [iOS and Android apps](/learn/mobile/).
