Turn a lesson into audio and learn while you walk, commute or rest your eyes. You pay 10 credits once per lesson and voice. Every replay after that is free.

## Listen

1. Open a level and choose **Theory**.
2. Press **Listen · 10** at the top of the theory.
3. Questie shows **Preparing audio** while the voice is made, then starts playing.

A lesson is one level's theory cards. The audio follows the cards: when a card's audio ends, the next card opens and keeps playing.

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Play / Pause | Starts or stops the audio, on the card you are on |
| Position | Scrub through the audio |
| Speed | Cycles 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x and 0.75x |
| Previous / Next, arrow keys | Move between cards; the audio jumps with you |
| Space | Play or pause |

Leaving the level stops the audio. The sound button in the top bar mutes game sounds; narration has its own controls.

## What you pay

| Case | Credits |
|---|---|
| First listen to a lesson in a voice | 10 |
| Replays of that lesson, on any device | Free |
| Other cards of the same lesson | Included |

Once you have paid for a lesson, the button reads **Listen**. Your unlock works on the web and in the [iOS and Android apps](/learn/mobile/), where audio keeps playing with the screen locked.

If the audio fails, you see **Audio failed · 10 credits refunded** and can press **Retry**. The refund shows in your [credit history](/learn/credits/) as `Refund: audio failed`.

## Self-hosting

Narration uses ElevenLabs. Set `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` and narration runs unmetered with your own key. See [Configuration](/self-host/config/).
