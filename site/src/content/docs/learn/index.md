Questie turns every course into a quest: short theory cards, challenges that cost lives, katas that run in your browser and a review queue that brings back what you missed. A free account gets you every library course, all games, katas, review and notes, plus one AI-built course.

Want to look first? [Try a level](/demo/#/c/http-basics) in the demo, no account needed.

## Create your account

1. Open the app and choose **Create account**.
2. Use **Continue with Google**, **Continue with Apple**, or your name, email and a password (8+ characters).
3. With email and password, open the link in the email we send you. Google and Apple accounts skip this step.

More on sign-in methods in [Account](/learn/account/).

## Pick your first course

A new account opens on **What do you want to learn?**

- Type a topic, pick a level and press **Build course · Free**. Questie writes a full course for you. See [Course from a topic](/ai/topic/).
- Or pick a course from the **Library** row, or **Browse library** for the full list.
- **Skip** takes you straight to your courses.

Your courses live under **Courses** in the top bar. A course joins that list as soon as you make progress in it.

## The course map

Opening a course shows its map: a winding path of levels.

- Levels open in order. Clear one and the next unlocks.
- The boss level sits at the end of the path.
- Stars under each level show your best run (up to 3).
- **Unlock all** opens every level at once, for when you already know the basics.
- **Dojo**, under the path, lists the course katas so you can practice them any time.
- **Mark completed** tells Questie you are done with a course. Its reviews stop until you press **Reopen**.

If the course has a mock exam, its button sits at the top of the map. See [Mock exams](/learn/mock/).

## Course tabs

Inside a course, the tabs under the top bar take you to:

| Tab | What you get |
|---|---|
| Map | Levels, katas and the mock exam |
| Arcade | Fast games built from the challenges you unlocked ([Games](/learn/games/)) |
| Review | Cards due today, with a count badge ([Review](/learn/review/)) |
| Notes | Your Markdown notes for this course ([Notes](/learn/notes/)) |
| Materials | Every image, video and audio file in the course, by level |
| Answers | Every written answer you gave, with scores, and **Export .md** |

The top bar also shows your rank, XP and combo flame. How those work: [Levels, XP and lives](/learn/levels/).

## Your progress follows you

Progress, answers and notes sync to your account, so you can switch between laptop, phone and the [iOS and Android apps](/learn/mobile/) and pick up where you left off.
