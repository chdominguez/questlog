## Run it yourself

One container, your own Anthropic key, every AI feature unmetered. App AGPL-3.0, course format MIT, library courses CC BY 4.0. [Self-host Questie](/self-host/docker/)

## Questions

### What is a credit?

The unit for AI work. Each AI action shows its cost on its button before you spend anything, and spends of 100 credits or more ask you to confirm.

### Do plan credits roll over?

Plan credits reset each billing month. Top-up credits never expire. Questie spends plan credits first, so your top-ups last.

### What if an AI build fails?

You get the credits back automatically, and the refund shows in your history.

### Is VAT included?

Yes. Prices include VAT.

### Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, in the billing portal. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

### Do app purchases share my balance?

Yes. One balance on the web, iOS and Android.

### What is always free?

Every library course, all games, katas, review and notes, and your first AI-built course from a topic.
